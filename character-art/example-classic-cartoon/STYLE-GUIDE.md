# Classic Cartoon — Example Style Guide

This folder exists to show how a finished style pack should be organized.

## Canvas
- 1024 × 1024 PNG
- transparent background
- do not crop individual pieces

## Character look
- friendly web-cartoon proportions
- larger-than-realistic head
- clean outlines
- simple shapes
- flat colours with minimal shading
- readable at small avatar sizes

## Artist workflow
1. Start with one of the base bodies.
2. Draw your item on a new transparent 1024 × 1024 canvas.
3. Keep the body temporarily visible underneath while drawing so placement is correct.
4. Hide the body before export.
5. Export only the item layer as PNG.
6. Put it in the matching numbered folder.
7. Build at least one finished character from your pieces to prove they layer correctly.

## Important
The filenames in this example are easy for humans to read on purpose.

The game/dev team can generate or maintain any technical metadata separately. Artists should not need to edit technical manifests for normal art work.
