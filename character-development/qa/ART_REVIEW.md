# Character Asset Art Review

Style implementations are reviewed against their own style guide and the shared canonical design.

- [ ] The item clearly represents the intended canonical item.
- [ ] The interpretation is visually consistent with this style.
- [ ] Scale is appropriate beside existing approved items in this style.
- [ ] Silhouette is readable.
- [ ] Body coverage looks intentional.
- [ ] Hair/headwear overlap works.
- [ ] Face accessories sit correctly.
- [ ] Clothing seams and overlaps look intentional.
- [ ] Line treatment matches the style.
- [ ] Shading/detail level matches the style.
- [ ] Color interpretation remains recognizable.
- [ ] The item works on every body it claims to support.
- [ ] Differences from other styles are artistic choices, not accidental changes to gameplay identity.

Different styles are allowed to look substantially different. The review is for coherence and compatibility, not visual sameness.
